See you at the market.

Our first season selling chicken started under a tent: Wednesday evenings in the field at Tilted Barn, Saturday mornings on the Village Green in North Scituate, Sundays at Shady Acres in Foster. That’s where most of you met us, tasted the difference, and came back for more.

The market season ends in October. The farm doesn’t. The hens keep laying through the winter, the freezers stay full, and we keep coming to you: Thursday delivery, pickup at the farm, and Saturday mornings on the Green, in the same corner where the tent stood. We’ll be back under it in June.
