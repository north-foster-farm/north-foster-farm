This policy covers every order from North Foster Farm, whether it
comes to you by delivery or by pickup. It says what you are buying,
how you pay, and what happens if you change your mind or something goes
wrong. How we get your order to you is in our
[delivery and pickup policy](/delivery-policy/).

## Placing an order

<!-- B1 -->
You order on the [order page](/order/). Your order is placed once your
payment goes through, and not before. We email you as soon as it is.

<!-- B2 Q16 -->
If we price something wrong, or can't fill your order, we may cancel
it. We refund it in full and tell you why.

## Prices

<!-- Q10 -->
Prices are in US dollars and are shown on the order page before you
pay. The order page shows your total, and that is what you are charged.

<!-- B3 -->
Your order keeps the prices and discounts in force the day you placed
it, even if they change before your pickup or delivery day.

### Weights

<!-- B4 Q5 -->
Whole chickens are sold in weight ranges, such as 2.5 to 2.9 pounds.
Each range has one price. You pay the price of the range you chose, and
we don't change it after your chicken is weighed. Eggs are sold by the
dozen.

### Discounts

<!-- B5 -->
Orders of enough dollars get a discount off the items. The tiers are
in the [delivery and pickup policy](/delivery-policy/#what-it-costs),
and they apply to every order, pickup orders included. The highest
tier you reach applies on its own.

<!-- B5 Q9 -->
Discounts never combine. If more than one applies, your order takes
whichever saves you more.

## How you pay

<!-- B1 -->
Orders are prepaid when you place them. You can pay by credit card,
Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash App Pay, or Venmo. Card, Apple Pay, Google
Pay, and Cash App Pay payments go through Square. Venmo payments go
through PayPal. We never see your card number.

## Items that sell out

<!-- B6 -->
The order page shows what is in stock. Two people can order the last
pack at nearly the same moment. If that happens to you, we cancel the
order we can't fill and refund it in full. We email you.

<!-- B6 Q11 -->
We don't substitute one item for another. If we can't fill your order
as placed, we ask you first.

## Changing or canceling an order

<!-- B8 -->
Sign in with the email address you ordered with. We email you a link
to sign in. There's no password. Your account page lets you change
the day, your pickup time, where we'll find your cooler, and your
notes, or cancel the order, until the cutoff:

- **Delivery:** {{< cutoff "delivery" >}}, the day before your
  {{< terms "day" >}}.
- **Scheduled pickup:** 11:59 PM the day before your pickup.
- **On-farm pickup:** 11:59 PM the day before your pickup.

<!-- B9 -->
To change what is in an order, use Change items on the account page.
It opens the order page, shows the new total, and charges or refunds
the difference.

<!-- B10 -->
If we have asked you to choose something, such as after a missed
delivery, you can change or cancel until you have answered.

<!-- B11 Q7 -->
After the cutoff, we have already started your order, and you can no
longer change or cancel it yourself. Call, text, or email us. We'll do
what we can, but we can't promise a refund.

## Refunds

<!-- B12 -->
When you cancel before the cutoff, we refund the order in full, the
way you paid. A Venmo payment is refunded to Venmo. It usually shows
within a few business days.

<!-- B13 -->
If we ever have to cancel an order, we refund it in full too. The one
exception is a delivery fee we keep, below.

<!-- B14 -->
If a refund doesn't go through on its own, we send it by hand and tell
you.

<!-- B15 -->
If you change an order and the new total is lower, we refund the
difference.

### The delivery fee after a missed delivery

<!-- B16 -->
If we can't complete a delivery because of something on your side, such
as no cooler out, no access to your address or no way to find it, we
keep the delivery fee. It pays for a trip we made, and it is not a
penalty. We may waive it. If you ask us to deliver again, the fee is
charged again.

<!-- B16 -->
Everything else in the order is refunded if you cancel. The
[delivery and pickup policy](/delivery-policy/#on-delivery-day) says
how long we hold the order and what your choices are.

## If something is wrong with your order

<!-- B17 Q2 Q3 Q4 -->
If an item is missing, damaged, the wrong one, or not what you
ordered, tell us within 48 hours of your pickup or delivery. Use
"Report a problem" on your account page, or email us. Say what
happened and which items. We read every report, and we'll make it
right with a replacement, a credit or a refund.

<!-- Q4 -->
A photo helps, but you don't need one.

## Food safety and handling

<!-- Q6 -->
Once your order is delivered or picked up, it's in your care. Put
chicken in the refrigerator or freezer, and eggs in the refrigerator,
as soon as you can. Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F.

<!-- Q6 -->
We can't take back food once it has left our hands, so we can't refund
a problem that comes from how it was kept or cooked afterwards.

## Your consent

By placing an order, you agree to this policy and to our
[delivery and pickup policy](/delivery-policy/).
