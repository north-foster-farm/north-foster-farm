# 3. Questions for James (#415)

Each has my recommendation, and the draft already uses it. Nothing
below is a policy until James says so. Not legal advice.

1. **Does this block launch?** The checkout asks people to agree to
   policy, and today it names only the delivery policy. Recommendation:
   yes, a short sales policy should ship before launch, with #405 listing
   it. Assumed: customers who pay online are bound by what the checkout
   links.

2. **How long after pickup or delivery can a customer report a
   problem?** The account page has no limit today. Recommendation:
   48 hours, since chicken and eggs are perishable. The draft says 48
   hours.

3. **What do you give, and who chooses?** Options: replacement, credit,
   refund. Recommendation: we offer all three and decide per case. The
   draft says "a replacement, a credit or a refund". The account page's
   Report a problem has no credit mechanism, so a credit is done by
   hand.

4. **What counts as a problem, and do you ask for a photo?** The draft
   says missing, damaged, wrong, or "not what you ordered", and that a
   photo helps but is not required. Do you want to name quality (taste,
   texture) or leave it out?

5. **Weight ranges.** Whole chickens are priced per range, not per
   pound. Recommendation: no repricing after weighing, and a bird outside
   its range is handled as a problem (Q3). The draft says so.

6. **Food safety and handling.** Is the chicken sold fresh or frozen?
   The draft says only to refrigerate or freeze and to cook to 165°F
   (the standard USDA figure). Do you want that guidance, a disclaimer
   that you can't take back food once it leaves you, or neither?

7. **After the cutoff.** The customer cannot cancel. Recommendation:
   say we "can't promise a refund" and decide case by case. The draft
   says that. Do you want a fixed rule instead, such as "no refunds
   after the cutoff"?

8. **The 7-day hold after a missed delivery.** What happens to the
   perishable order in those 7 days? The delivery policy says "we hold
   your order." If it can't be kept fresh, the hold needs to shrink or
   the wording must say the farm may discard it. The draft leaves the
   live wording as is.

9. **Discount terms.** Groups (Friends and family 10%, Wholesale 20%) and
   codes exist, and passes look like ordinary codes. Do you want terms
   such as one code per order, or no cash value? The draft says only that
   discounts never combine.

10. **Sales tax.** I found no tax in the code. If you charge none
    because the products are exempt, is that worth a line? The draft
    says only "prices are in US dollars". Check with an accountant.

11. **Substitutions.** The code never substitutes. Is "we don't
    substitute and ask you first" the rule you want? The draft says so.

12. **Square and PayPal.** Card networks and processors commonly expect
    a merchant to publish a refund or return policy, contact details,
    the currency and a delivery policy. I did not check Square's or
    PayPal's current seller terms (no network use in a cloud lane).
    Please confirm against both agreements. The draft covers refunds,
    contact details, currency and delivery.

13. **Where to link.** Confirmation email, cancel email, account page
    cancel panel and packing slip. Recommendation: all four, as one
    plain line, "Sales policy". Section 4 only, after approval.

14. **Name, and the lawyer.** "Sales policy" (recommended) or "Terms of
    sale"? Do you want a lawyer to read it for liability, governing law
    (Rhode Island), and disputes before it goes live? I left all three
    out of the draft.

15. **Pickup not collected.** Nothing in the code handles a customer who
    doesn't show for a booked pickup. The draft is silent. Should it say
    the order is not refunded, or held, or left to you?

16. **Price errors.** The draft lets the farm cancel and refund an order
    it priced wrong or can't fill. Is that what you want, or should the
    price on the order page always hold?
