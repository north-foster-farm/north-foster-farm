## When we deliver

- Deliveries run on {{< terms "days" >}}, roughly from
  {{< terms "window" "delivery" >}}. The window is approximate.
- Order by {{< cutoff "delivery" >}} for that {{< terms "day" >}}'s
  delivery. After the cutoff, the order page offers the
  {{< terms "day" >}} after.
- We don't deliver on a holiday. When one falls on a
  {{< terms "day" >}}, such as Thanksgiving, the order page skips that
  week.

## What it costs

Delivery is available for orders of {{< terms "minimum" >}} or more,
after discounts. The delivery fee is {{< terms "fee" >}}, waived when
your items come to {{< terms "waived-at" >}} or more.

{{% bulk-tiers %}}

Bulk discounts apply to every order, pickup orders included. The
highest tier you reach applies, automatically.
Discounts never combine: if more than one applies, your order takes
whichever saves you more.

A Rhode Island address outside our delivery area (below) costs
{{< terms "outside-fee" >}} more, on every order, even one of
{{< terms "waived-at" >}} or more. The order page shows
it as soon as you enter your ZIP code.

## On delivery day

Leave a cooler with ice outside on {{< terms "day" >}} morning,
somewhere easy to find. The order page asks where it will be, and for
a phone number so we can reach you on the day. Add a gate or door
code to your notes if we'll need one.

We email you a reminder the evening before.

If we can't make it due to unforeseen circumstances, such as
inclement weather, we'll notify you via email and postpone your
delivery until the following {{< terms "day" >}}. Alternatively, you
can choose to pick up your order at the farm or at a scheduled pickup,
or cancel your order for a full refund.

You don't need to be home. If there's no cooler where you told us, we
can't get to that spot, or we can't find your address as you gave it,
we'll try to reach you by phone, text or the doorbell. If we can't
reach you, we won't leave your order. You can choose to have it
delivered the following {{< terms "day" >}}, pick it up at the farm or at
a scheduled pickup, or cancel your order for a refund of your items. We
hold your order for {{< terms "hold" >}} while you choose a new way to
get your order. If you haven't chosen after {{< terms "hold" >}}, we
cancel your order, refund your items, and send you an email to confirm
the cancellation.

If we couldn't complete a delivery because there was no cooler out, no
access to the address you gave us, or no way to find the address as
you gave it, we keep the delivery fee, including any charge for an
address outside our delivery area. It pays for a trip we made, and it
is not a penalty. We may choose to waive it. A second delivery
attempt the following {{< terms "day" >}} is charged the fee again.

Once it's delivered, it's in your care.

## Delivery area

These are the towns we deliver to at the fees above. You may provide
any other Rhode Island ZIP code that is *not* in our delivery area for
{{< terms "outside-fee" >}} more. If we can't get to your address,
we'll call, text, or email you. If we aren't able to get your order to
you, we will hold your order for up to {{< terms "hold" >}}, as
described in "On delivery day" above. For anywhere else,
{{% company "email" "email us" %}} before you order.

## On-farm pickup

There is no minimum or fee. On-farm pickup occurs at
{{< terms "farm-name" >}}, located at {{< terms "farm-address" >}}. The order
page lists the time slots we have available for pickup in the coming
one to two weeks. You select your pick-up time when you place your
order. If we can't accommodate your pick-up appointment, we'll cancel
your order, refund it in full, and send you an email to confirm the
cancellation.

## Scheduled pickup

Scheduled pickup is pickup at a place and a time we set: the drop
site, a market or a pop-up. There is no minimum or fee.

**The drop site** in {{< terms "town" "drop" >}} runs on
{{< terms "days" "drop" >}} from
{{< terms "window" "drop" >}}, {{< terms "starting" "drop" >}}on
the {{< terms "place" "drop" >}}: the same corner of the lot where
our tent sits in market season.

**Markets and pop-ups** are opened one event at a time. When we open
one, it appears on the order page, with its place and hours, and you
can choose it there. The place and hours are the event's own. We
don't open every market or pop-up we attend.

You can order for a pickup through the end of next week. Order by
11:59 PM the day before the pickup you choose.
