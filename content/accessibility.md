We want everyone to be able to use this site, order from us and reach
us. If something here gets in your way, tell us and we'll fix it, or
take your order another way in the meantime.

## Standards

We aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)] 2.1
at level AA. The site has not been formally assessed.

[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)]: https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/

## How the site works

- **Product search.** It opens with the Search products button, or
  Command-K or Control-K. The arrow keys move through the results,
  Enter adds one to your cart and Escape closes it.
- **The order page.** Every field has a label. If something needs
  fixing when you place your order, we say what and move you to it.
  The delivery cutoff countdown stays quiet for screen readers.
- **Signing in.** There's no password. We email you a link, which
  works once within 15 minutes.
- **Video.** Our clips are short and silent. They play only while
  they're on screen, and not at all if your device asks for reduced
  motion or to save data. Every video has a pause button, and a video
  you pause stays paused. Once you pause the video at the top of the
  home page, you can turn off autoplay for the whole site.
- **The delivery map.** It skips its animation under reduced motion.
  What it shows is also in text: the list of places beside it, the ZIP
  code checker, and the table of towns in
  [our delivery policy](/delivery-policy/#delivery-area).

## What we know falls short

- The map pans only by dragging, and a screen reader may not reach its
  pins. Its list of places and the table of towns hold the same
  information.
- Card and wallet payments run in fields and buttons from Square, and
  Venmo in a button from PayPal. We don't control how accessible they
  are. If one doesn't work for you, call or text us and we'll help you
  place your order.
