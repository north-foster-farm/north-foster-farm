  The Scituate market season is over; find us at the Foster Farmers’ Market in
  October, then at our Saturday egg drop in North Scituate.
---

October 5th was the final day of the Scituate Rotary Farmers Market
2024 season. Thank you to everyone who stopped by our booth and made
our first farmers market experience so successful!

We will be participating in the
[Foster Farmers’ Market](https://fosterfarmersmarketri.com) on Sunday,
October 13th and Sunday, October 27th, from 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM. The
Foster Farmers’ Market is located at Shady Acres Diner (164 Danielson
Pike in Foster).

After the 2024 farmers market season ends, our eggs will be available
at our Saturday drop site at the Scituate Rotary Farmers Market
location (46 Institute Lane in North Scituate). We will be on site from
10 to 11 AM with fresh eggs.

Although we have asked our hens to do their best, eggs are limited to
supply on hand and offered on a first come first served basis.
