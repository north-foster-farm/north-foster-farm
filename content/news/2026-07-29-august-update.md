We can’t believe it’s August already. The summer has gone by so
quickly—it feels like we were setting up the brooder a couple of weeks
ago, and here we are already past the halfway point of the market
season. Things are going great out on the farm.

If we haven’t met yet: we’re **North Foster Farm**, a small family farm
in Foster, Rhode Island. We raise chickens on grass,
[move them to fresh pasture regularly][pasture], and feed them grain
that’s [free of GMOs][gmo]. We think that’s how chickens should be
raised—and we think you can taste the difference.

This week we moved another batch of chicks out of the brooder and onto
pasture, which is one of our favorite days of the season. They spend
their first few weeks indoors under heat lamps, and once they’re fully
feathered they graduate to the field. From then on they live in mobile
pens that we move onto fresh grass every day.

{{< figure src="images/tilted-market.jpg"

Our freezers are fully stocked, and right now we have the whole lineup
on hand:

- **Eggs by the dozen**
- **Whole chickens**
- **Cornish hens**
- **Boneless breasts**
- **Bone-in thighs**
- **Tenders**
- **Drumsticks**
- **Wings**
- **Chicken sausage** (maple breakfast and sweet Italian)
- **Livers**

Everything is available at all three of our weekly farmers markets.
We’d love for you to stop by, say hello, and see what we’ve got.

We’re also getting close to something a lot of you have asked us about:
**delivery**. If you’re interested, please [send us an email][delivery]
and we’ll get you more information. We’ll publish full details on the
website soon.

{{< figure src="images/scituate-market.jpg"
  alt="Vendor tents lining the Village Green in North Scituate"

This was our first year selling our own chicken, and honestly, we
weren’t sure what to expect. What means the most is how many of you
came back to tell us how it tasted—and then bought more. If you’re old
enough to remember chicken before it became a “manufactured” product,
you probably know what we mean. As we like to say, pasture-raised
chicken tastes like _chicken_.

Thank you to everyone who has supported our farm this year. We just
can’t say “thank you” enough. We’ve got more good things coming soon!

— The NFF team
