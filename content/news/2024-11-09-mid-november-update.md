The 2024 farmers market season has ended for us, but our eggs are still
available! You will find us at our Saturday drop site at the Scituate
Rotary Farmers Market location (46 Institute Lane in North Scituate).
We will be on site from 10 to 11 AM with fresh eggs.

Although we have asked our hens to do their best, eggs are limited to
supply on hand and offered on a first come first served basis.
