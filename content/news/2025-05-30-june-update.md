The 2025 farmers market season is finally here! Here's where you'll
find us on the weekends:

- **[Scituate Rotary Farmers Market][scituate]**\
  **When:** every Saturday\
  **Time:** 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM\
  **Where:** 46 Institute Lane, Scituate, RI 02857
- **[Foster Farmers' Market][foster]**\
  **When:** every Sunday\
  **Time:** 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM\
  **Where:** 164 Danielson Pike, Foster, RI 02825

We offer **pasture-raised, non-GMO chicken eggs** by the dozen. Stop by
our booth to purchase eggs, learn about our farm, and find out why we
believe pasture-raised chickens lay the best eggs.
