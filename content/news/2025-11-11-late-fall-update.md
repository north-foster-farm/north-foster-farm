Thank you for a great 2025 season!

Before we say goodbye to 2025, the Foster Farmers’ Market will be
hosting several pop-up events in the coming weeks:

- **Holiday Gifts, Greenery, and Tasty Goodies**\
  **When:** Sunday, November 30\
  **Time:** 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM\
  **Where:** Shady Acres Diner, 164 Danielson Pike, Foster, RI
- **A Very Merry Market**\
  **When:** Sunday, December 14\
  **Time:** 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM\
  **Where:** Foster Center Volunteer Fire Co., 86 Foster Center Road,
  Foster, RI

We hope to see you there!

We are looking forward to an even busier 2026 and if all goes well, we
should have some exciting announcements about additional offerings
soon.

Our markets are closed, but the eggs keep coming and they taste just as
good when there’s snow on the ground. We hope you are able to take
advantage of our Saturday morning egg drops throughout the winter
months. We’ll be at the site of the Scituate Rotary Farmers Market, 46
Institute Lane, Saturday mornings from 10 to 11.

We’ll email with as much advanced notice if something comes up that
will prevent us from being in Scituate on any given Saturday.

We hope you are continuing to enjoy our eggs and will spread the word
to family and friends who may also be interested in shopping with us
Saturday mornings.

Please visit
[@northfosterfarm on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/northfosterfarm)
for pictures of our farm to see the Who, and the How behind our
delicious eggs!
