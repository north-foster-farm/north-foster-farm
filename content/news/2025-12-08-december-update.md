  Holiday demand is up, so we skip the December 14 pop-up to fill orders;
  Saturday egg drops at the Scituate site continue all winter.
---

With the arrival of the holiday season, we’ve seen an increase in
demand for our eggs. Our hens are working hard to keep up! Due to the
combination of high demand and a seasonal slowdown in production, we’ve
decided to focus on filling orders this week rather than attending the
pop-up market on Sunday, December 14.

We will, however, be at the Scituate Rotary Farmers Market site (46
Institute Lane) this Saturday morning from 10 to 11 with as many eggs
as our team can gather.

Our Saturday-morning egg drops will continue throughout the winter
months. They taste just as good when there’s snow on the ground!

Thank you to all our customers for supporting us this year. Please
consider spreading the word to family and friends who may also be
interested in shopping with us Saturday mornings.

Please visit
[@northfosterfarm on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/northfosterfarm)
for pictures of our farm to see the Who, and the How behind our
delicious eggs!
