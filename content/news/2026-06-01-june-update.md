It’s been quiet here on the website these past few months, but not out
on the farm. The grass has come back, the chickens are out rotating
through our pastures, the brooder is full of peeping chicks, and the
market season is finally here.

If we haven’t met yet: we’re **North Foster Farm**, a small family farm
in Foster, Rhode Island. We raise chickens on grass,
[move them to fresh pasture regularly][pasture], and feed them grain
that’s [free of GMOs][gmo]. We think that’s how chickens should be
raised—and we think you can taste the difference.

This year, we’re proud to be selling our own pastured eggs and pastured
chicken. **Eggs by the dozen** and **Cornish hens** are in stock right
now. The rest of our lineup—whole chickens, boneless breasts, bone-in
thighs, drumsticks, tenders, and wings—will be in our freezers later
this week and available for purchase by Saturday, June 6th.

{{< figure src="images/chicks.jpg"
  alt="New chicks settling in under the heat lamps in the brooder" >}}
Heat lamps for now, but in just a few weeks, they’ll be fully feathered
and ready for pasture.
{{< /figure >}}

You can find us at three farmers markets every week. We’d love for you
to stop by, say hello, and see what we’ve got.

- **[Summer Market at Tilted Barn][tilted]:**
  - Wednesdays
  - 4:00 PM - 7:00 PM
  - Tilted Barn Brewery, One Hemsley Place, Exeter, RI
- **[Scituate Rotary Farmers Market][scituate]:**
  - Saturdays
  - 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM
  - The Village Green, 46 Institute Lane, North Scituate, RI
- **[Foster Farmers Market][foster]:**
  - Sundays
  - 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM
  - Shady Acres Diner, 164 Danielson Pike, Foster, RI

In addition to our weekly markets, you’ll find our tent at a handful of
summer pop-up events around Warwick from July into September. We’ll
share the whole schedule in a future update—check back soon!

As always, we can’t say “thank you” enough. Your support is what lets
us keep doing this, and we can’t wait to see you at market this summer.

— The NFF team
