---
title: "Update: New Farmers Market Schedule"
date: 2024-09-12
description: >-
  Eggs are selling out early, so we move to an every-other-week schedule at the
  Scituate Rotary Farmers Market for the rest of 2024.
---

Due to the unexpected, but greatly appreciated demand for our pasture
raised eggs we have been selling out fairly early each week at the
farmers market. We recently doubled the size of our flock, but the new
recruits won’t be laying sellable eggs for a few more weeks.

With this in mind we’ve decided to go to an every other week schedule
for the rest of the season. We look forward to seeing you at the
Scituate Rotary Farmers Market on the following Saturdays:

- September 14
- September 28
- October 5 (the final 2024 market day)

Please check back here for updates about how to purchase eggs after
October 5th until the 2025 market opens.
